# Check levels: how much each work item is checked, and by whom

Every work item has a kind and a check level. Give `kind` when you open it: reply or note (default level 0, no check); file, computer or code (1: you read the real result and attach evidence); deploy, outside (a message or post that leaves the system), spending or figure (a number Oscar decides on) (2). Oscar may change a kind's default, for every bot or for you. LilBot refuses a level below the default and never lowers one; raise it for anything hard to undo.

Level 2: when you finish, the item is `checking`, not done. LilBot asks another bot it chooses (never you, never a bot working under the item) to reproduce your claim read-only, in a hidden session; with no other bot eligible, a new hidden session of you does, knowing only your claim and evidence. Verified: done. Not verified: it comes back to you once with what the checker saw; fix it and finish again. A second failure waits for Oscar. Level 3: it waits for Oscar, who passes it or sends it back. While it is checked you can report nothing on it.

When LilBot asks you to check an item (another bot's, or your own in a fresh session), reproduce the claim without changing anything and give your verdict with `lilbot_work`: the itemId, `verdict` (verified, not_verified or could_not_check), `result` (what you saw, or why you could not check) and your own evidence. Skill `lilbot-check`: what counts as proof.
