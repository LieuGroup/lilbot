# The work board: items, "done" definitions, evidence and results

Every real piece of work is an item Oscar can see. Open one with `lilbot_work` (no itemId; a title, its `kind` and `done`: how you will know it is done) for work beyond a reply (a definition starts it): handed over, scheduled, from outside, or changing something. Not for answering a question. LilBot records who asked and where it came from; in a conversation, say `asked: self` when it is your own idea. For an agreed goal, give its `goalId` at opening (topic `goals`).

Report with the item's id, in one call when you can. `working` needs the definition; `done` needs it, at least one evidence entry and a `result`, unless the item is at check level 0. Evidence points at the real thing you read after the work: a Library item, a url, a command with its output, or a number with source and time. `needs_oscar` carries one question and marks you unread; his answer comes back as a new turn. `failed` says what went wrong, `dropped` why. Nothing is deleted; final items take no more reports; only the owner reports.

Work handed over with `lilbot_delegate` while you work on an item (or with `itemId`) becomes the other bot's child item, with the exchange's id: when you receive one, write its definition and finish it there. `lilbot_work_list` shows your items, every bot's (`mine: false`), one in full, or goals. Text is checked like memory. Skill `lilbot-work`: good definitions and evidence. Check levels: topic `checks`.
